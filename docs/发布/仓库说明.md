### 英文

```markdown
The first version of Don't Delete Me, for macOS.

Every icon on your desktop gets a face. They watch your cursor, and they really don't want to go in the Trash.

### What they do

- Follow the pointer with their eyes, and blink now and then
- Blush and look away when you hover over them
- Get dizzy when you shake them around
- Sweat, tremble, and finally cry as you drag them toward the Trash — while the other icons cover their eyes, peek between their fingers, or stare
- Leave a little ghost behind when they are deleted
- Doze off if the file hasn't been modified for more than half a year
- Get bored and look around when the mouse is left alone
- Turn to look when something new lands on the desktop

One menu bar icon, two items: Pause / Resume and Quit. Works across two displays.

### Before you download

- Apple Silicon only. Declared minimum is macOS 14, but it has only been tested on macOS 27.0.1.
- The app is not notarized, so macOS will block it the first time you open it. The README explains how to open it. [TO VERIFY · 待核实: link to the README section once the steps are confirmed]
- It asks for one permission — to control Finder — so it can find out where your icons are. It only reads; it never moves, renames, or deletes anything. It never goes online.

### Known limitations

No faces while Stacks is on. Faces can cover image thumbnails; tears can cover part of the file name. No ghost for "Delete Immediately". The full list is in the README.

Windows is planned, not implemented.
```
